# Vice City Forums — Growth Operating System

Last updated: 2026-09-09

## Position

Vice City Forums is the independent, source-first GTA 6 community: confirmed news stays separate from theory, clips keep their provenance, and every useful guide leads into a real public discussion.

Primary promise: **Know what is confirmed. Debate everything else.**

Primary conversion: **Complete a member profile and join one discussion.**

## The content flywheel

1. Verify a GTA 6 development against Rockstar or another direct first-party source.
2. Update the matching canonical page and its visible “last checked” date.
3. Open one staff-authored forum thread with a specific question, not a copied press release.
4. Produce one vertical short, one still/carousel, and one text post from the same verified fact.
5. Link each platform post to the matching guide with a channel-specific UTM tag.
6. Quote and credit the best real community response in the next recap, with permission where required.
7. Measure guide visits, completed profiles, posts, replies, and returning members—not follower count alone.

## Short-form format

Use a repeatable 20–35 second structure:

1. **0–2 seconds:** one concrete tension—“Rockstar confirmed this; the internet added the rest.”
2. **2–12 seconds:** show the first-party source name and the exact confirmed detail.
3. **12–24 seconds:** show the most useful implication or unresolved question.
4. **24–30 seconds:** ask one binary or ranked question.
5. **Final frame:** `VICECITYFORUMS.COM` plus the matching page title.

Always label official footage, user gameplay, fan art, edited concepts, and AI-generated imagery correctly. Preserve creator credit and source URLs.

## Distribution rules

- Point each post at the deepest relevant guide; use the homepage only for general brand posts.
- Keep one canonical domain everywhere: `https://vicecityforums.com/`.
- Do not buy followers, mass-DM strangers, scrape contact lists, impersonate Rockstar, or seed fake conversations.
- Do not use the Rockstar star logo as the forum's identity. Prefer the original Vice City Forums mark and OG card in `assets/`.
- Pitch genuinely useful guides to GTA creators, fan newsletters, gaming podcasters, community Discords, and resource lists individually. Explain why that specific page helps their audience.
- Ask permission before republishing a creator's clip, quote, screenshot, or artwork.

## Weekly review

Every seven days, keep the three topics with the strongest combination of search impressions, engaged visits, completed profiles, and real replies. Rewrite weak hooks, strengthen thin pages with first-party evidence, and retire formats that attract views without community activity.

“Famous” is the outcome. The operating target is a trusted page people find, a sharp post they share, and a discussion worth returning to.
