The six major Leonida destinations

Rockstar's official Only in Leonida guide profiles six destinations. These are the confirmed backbone of the world—not a complete street map.

Official destination · Urban

Vice City

The neon-soaked city at the center of Leonida, with beaches, nightlife, neighborhoods, a port, and a wider urban sprawl.

Official destination · Islands

Leonida Keys

An island chain tied to Jason's history and Brian Heder's boat-yard operation. The exact island-by-island layout is not officially mapped.

Official destination · Wetlands

Grassrivers

Leonida's wetland country, presented by Rockstar as a distinct natural environment.

Official destination · Coast

Port Gellhorn

A named coastal destination beyond Vice City. Its exact boundaries and internal district layout remain unconfirmed.

Official destination · Industry

Ambrosia

An inland industrial destination associated with the Allied Crystal sugar refinery in Rockstar's world material.

Official destination · North

Mount Kalaga

A northern natural area associated with outdoor terrain and a different pace from Vice City's urban core.

Vice City: named neighborhoods and landmarks

Rockstar location-page names: Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka Flea Market, and the VC Port. Rockstar describes Ocean Beach's art-deco hotels and sands, Little Cuba's panaderías, the flea market's bootleg brands, and the port's cruise-ship traffic.

Official trailer signage and other first-party references: Stockyard, Downtown, Southside Vice City, Peacock Bay, SW 9th Ave, VCI Airport, and Kelly County. These names appear in official promotional material; signage does not by itself establish exact borders or coordinates.

Location evidence labels

Official destination means Rockstar profiles the place. Officially named means a Rockstar page, store listing, or official trailer names it. Trailer signage means the name is readable in official footage. Unplaced means the name is real but its exact position is not established. Community theory is never presented as Rockstar-confirmed geography.

Has Rockstar released the full GTA 6 map?

Rockstar's public world pages identify destinations and selected place names, but do not provide a complete, coordinate-accurate map of Leonida. Fan reconstructions can help organize evidence, but their borders, distances, road connections, and inferred locations must remain labeled as community work.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

There is no definitive square-mile measurement in the Rockstar location material cited here. We will not publish a precise size or place pins from leaked or unverified map images as if they were official.

Frequently asked questions

Rockstar names Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, Tisha-Wocka Flea Market, and the VC Port; official trailer signage also shows names including Stockyard, Downtown, Southside, Peacock Bay, and VCI Airport.
