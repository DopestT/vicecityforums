# Vice City Forums — Advertising & Acquisition Sprint

Last updated: 2026-09-23

## Objective

Create visible daily forum activity and convert GTA VI interest into real visits, completed member profiles, threads, replies, and returning members.

## Non-negotiables

- Do not fabricate members, replies, likes, views, or engagement.
- Staff may start legitimate discussion threads under the real administrator account.
- No exact or obvious near-duplicate social image within 7 days on the same channel.
- Label official, user-gameplay, fan-made, edited, and AI-generated media accurately.
- Every promotion should link to the deepest relevant Vice City Forums page, not automatically the homepage.
- No paid spend without explicit budget approval.

## Daily operating loop

### Forum activity
- Publish 1 fresh staff-started discussion every day.
- Avoid duplicates against recent threads.
- Prefer one specific, opinion-driven question.
- Reply substantively to every legitimate member response that deserves a reply.
- Pin no more than 1–2 timely threads at once.

### Owned distribution
Publish the same core idea in channel-native form:
- 2 short-form video hooks per day.
- 1 image/poll/story per day.
- 2 text-led social posts per day.
- 1 direct forum CTA per day.

Each post gets:
- One clear hook.
- One question.
- One VCF destination URL.
- Channel-specific UTM tags.
- A source/provenance label when media is used.

### Community acquisition
Every day:
- Identify 3 relevant GTA VI conversations or creator posts where VCF can add something useful.
- Engage normally first; do not spam-drop links.
- Share a VCF link only when it directly answers the discussion or when self-promotion is allowed.
- Track communities/creators contacted and responses.

## Advertising funnel

### Stage 1 — Organic proof
Run the daily loop until at least 6–10 distinct creatives have measurable results.

Track:
- Hook hold / short-form completion rate
- Link clicks
- Landing-page visits
- Completed profiles
- New thread/reply activity
- 7-day returning visitors

### Stage 2 — Paid creative test
Only after explicit budget approval.

Start with the strongest 3 organic creatives.
Suggested test structure:
- 3 creatives
- 2 audiences per creative
- 3–5 day test
- Optimize toward landing-page visits or completed registrations, not impressions alone

Stop weak creatives early. Scale only the ads that produce engaged forum visits or registrations.

### Stage 3 — Retargeting
Retarget recent site visitors and video viewers with:
- “Join the debate” creative
- “Founding Citizen” creative
- Best-performing topic of the week

## Weekly creator outreach

Pitch 10 highly relevant GTA creators, fan newsletters, podcasters, Discord/community moderators, or resource-page owners.

Pitch format:
1. Mention their specific content/community.
2. Give one useful VCF page relevant to their audience.
3. Explain why it helps.
4. Ask for feedback, a resource mention, or permission to collaborate.

No mass template spam.

## Weekly review

Every 7 days:
- Keep the top 3 topic/creative combinations.
- Rewrite hooks on weak-but-useful topics.
- Drop formats that generate views but no forum activity.
- Update the next 7-day topic rotation.
- Report: visits, registrations, posting members, replies, return visits, and top acquisition source.

## Immediate execution queue

1. Publish one new staff discussion today.
2. Turn that thread into one 20–35 second short and one text post.
3. Use a unique VCF visual not used in the prior 7 days.
4. Publish one direct community invitation pointing to the matching forum thread/page.
5. Record the source/UTM and outcome.
6. Repeat tomorrow with a new topic.
